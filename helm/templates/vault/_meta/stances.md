# Stances

Your answers to questions that keep coming up in document reviews. A review chip reads this before it asks you anything. A stance only covers the client and scope on its row. Anything outside that gets a question, and your answer becomes a new row.

Stances hold until you change them, so the weekly upkeep never ages them out.
