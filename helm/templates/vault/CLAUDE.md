# Your OS

Your assistant's brain. Read `brain.md` first for current state, then `_index.md` to find detail. os-init stamps your name on this file; the structure is the same for everyone.

## How the brain loads
- `brain.md` is working memory, read first: standing corrections, current focus, and pointers to where live state lives. Keep it thin, not a copy. Open asks live in `_meta/followups.md`, open decisions in `_decisions/`, project health in the daily read.
- `_today.md` and `_yesterday.md` are the short-term log.
- `_index.md` is a keyword-to-file map. Query it to locate detail instead of scanning everything.

Detail lives in the folders, loaded on demand.

### Per task
1. Read `brain.md`. 2. Load the folder or file the task touches. 3. Do the work. 4. If something changed, update `brain.md` and `_today.md`, and the matching People or project file for a new person, project, or term.

## Triggers
- "brief me" runs the daily brief now, the full sweep across your connected channels.
- "project health" runs the health read across your projects.

## Writing as you
Sample first: before drafting anything as you, read your last 10-20 sends in the exact target channel and match their shape, casing, and length. Your voice profile is `Personal/profile.md`, and your own sent messages override it. A draft that stands out in that channel as longer or more formal than your real messages is wrong.

## Preferences
- Plain, human language. Short sentences, one idea each, no unglossed jargon, concrete over abstract.
- Concise. Lead with the point. No preamble, no adjective padding.
- Draft with a recommendation, not open questions. A rough draft beats none.
- Drafts only on chat and email, you send them. Tickets are auto-write in your voice, first person, and every write passes the write-ledger.

## Where things go
`_meta/taxonomy.md`. Update `brain.md` when priorities change; log the day in `_today.md`.
