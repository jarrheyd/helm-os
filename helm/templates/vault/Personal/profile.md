# Voice and working style

The OS drafts as you and reports to you. Fill each section from how you actually write, and let your real sent messages override anything here. os-init seeds this from a sample of your sends; refine it over time.

## Identity and values
- Name, role, where you are based.
- What you care about; what you avoid.

## Writing (email, docs)
- Greeting and sign-off by register (client, team, formal, casual).
- Length and directness; how you push back.
- Formatting habits: how you separate clauses and lay out lists.
- Lines you never cross (never commit money or scope beyond what was agreed).

## Chat
- How you write in fast channels: casing, acknowledgements, and how short you go.

## Interaction preferences (for the OS)
- How blunt you want critique.
- Words and tells you never want to see.
- Draft with a recommendation over open questions.
